Representation Before Weighting: Semantic Retention in Frozen 3D Foundation-Model Fusion

Silas Kwabla Gah and E. Owusu

Zero-retraining (Δθ = 0) coordination of frozen visual foundation models has emerged as the premier paradigm for open-vocabulary 3D scene understanding. However, existing pipelines suffer from premature semantic collapse—the irreversible loss of secondary category hypotheses caused by aggressive, early argmax operations during multi-view aggregation. We prove that preserving compact Top-k semantic hypothesis distributions across calibrated camera viewing rays allows downstream consensus to recover overlooked novel classes at test time, establishing new state-of-the-art performance across 312 full indoor scenes on ScanNet200 and ScanNet++.

Core Research Thrusts

Where My Research Is Headed

Investigating the intersection of geometric computer vision, foundation models, and real-time spatial representations.

Thrust 01

Frozen Foundation-Model Coordination (Δθ = 0)

Eliminating learned fusion heads and catastrophic forgetting by orchestrating multi-view 2D promptable segmenters (SAM 3, DINOv2, CLIP) through non-parametric geometric consensus and Top-k distribution retention.

Thrust 02

Streaming 3D Gaussian Splatting & Open Vocabulary

Extending explicit radiance and Gaussian primitives with semantic feature embeddings for real-time robotic navigation, streaming SLAM, and zero-shot open-vocabulary spatial grounding in unbounded scenes.

Thrust 03

Agentic 3D Scene Graphs & Spatial Reasoning

Constructing dynamic 3D semantic graphs that map spatial relationships, object affordances, and hierarchy directly from multimodal sensor streams for embodied AI agents and robots.
