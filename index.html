3D Geometric Vision & Foundation Models ACCV 2026

Training-Free 3D Point-Cloud Segmentation via Multi-View Consensus

Engineered a complete training-free 3D semantic segmentation pipeline connecting dense point-cloud representations (RegionPLC) with sparse 2D promptable segmenters (SAM 3). Implemented camera ray-casting and geometric 2D-to-3D lifting using calibrated intrinsics and poses $(K_j, T_j^{cw})$, followed by a non-parametric cross-view consensus rule to suppress projection noise without learned fusion networks.

+9.62 pts HM Gain (ScanNet++)
Evaluated across 312 full indoor scenes (~50M points) on ScanNet200 and ScanNet++ under $\Delta\theta=0$.

RCASA: Reliability-Calibrated Adaptive Semantic Arbitration

Formulated and implemented a general probabilistic framework for coordinating heterogeneous, frozen foundation models at inference time. Designed controlled Top-$k$ semantic retention experiments that identified and mitigated premature semantic collapse, enabling continuous distribution-level arbitration that recovers discarded class hypotheses without gradient updates.

+6.40 pts Harmonic Mean
Platt confidence calibration + contextual reliability learning with paired bootstrap 95% CIs [5.24, 7.64].
PyTorch Probability Calibration Scikit-Learn GroundingDINO SAM 2.1 CUDA / Triton

Multimodal Reinforcement Learning (RLVR) SACAIR 2026

Label-Free Process Rewards for Grounded Multimodal RL

Developed an intrinsic process-level grounding reward for visual reasoning using physical consensus across independent 3D and image perception sources. Built an end-to-end online GRPO/VERL training pipeline for Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct with vLLM rollout generation, eliminating reliance on human reward labels and stress-testing against reward-hacking vulnerabilities.

Area Bias $\rho$: 0.72 → 0.334
Validated on 1,000+ multimodal reasoning rollouts; verified grounding without degradation of final-answer accuracy.
PyTorch vLLM VERL GRPO Qwen2.5-VL-7B RLVR

Open-Vocabulary 2D Segmentation WACV 2027

OpenV-GFSS: Open-Vocabulary Generalized Few-Shot Segmentation

Developed a fully training-free open-vocabulary segmentation framework that composes promptable segmentation proposals with vision-language embeddings and support-set centroids. Conducted systematic ablation of spatial coordinate transformations, documenting aspect-ratio letterboxing versus square-stretch distortion as a key reproducibility confound.

63.58% HM on PASCAL-5i
Strong zero-shot baseline performance without task-specific parameter fine-tuning on COCO-20i.
